Extreme Hub Manager

Control maintenance for supported Extreme Hub apps. Maintenance stops playback and blocks navigation without signing customers out.

Administrator sign in

Turning maintenance on interrupts connected customers within about 10–15 seconds. Older app versions and offline devices cannot receive the command. Devices that already received it remain blocked offline until they reconnect and receive an explicit “off” status.

Turning maintenance off reopens the app automatically. Playback does not restart automatically. Changes are recorded with your administrator username.
